## 🎨 Design Analysis

### Visual Hierarchy
1. **Hero photo** draws immediate attention
2. **Large burgundy cards** anchor major sections
3. **White text on burgundy** provides excellent contrast
4. **Cream background** creates warm, elegant feel
5. **Floral accents** guide eye through page

### Responsive Strategy
- **Mobile-first approach** with progressive enhancement
- **Flexible widths** (88% of viewport)
- **Consistent max-widths** for readability
- **Maintained aspect ratios** for images
- **Touch-optimized** button sizes and spacing

### UX Patterns
- **Linear narrative flow** (top to bottom storytelling)
- **Clear section separation** with consistent spacing
- **Interactive elements clearly indicated** (buttons have distinct styling)
- **Progressive disclosure** (cover → full content)
- **Social features** (guestbook encourages engagement)
